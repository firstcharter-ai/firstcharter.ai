A clear starting point

Know what’s included before you commit.

Start with a complimentary conversation about what you need. Then choose a first engagement small enough to assess whether the help is useful.

You’ll receive a scope and price before committing. After the first piece of work, decide together whether more sessions or continuing support would help.

What does the initial consultation cover?

Your goals, the tasks you want help with, your experience with AI, and a sensible starting point. Hands-on training and implementation would follow under an agreed paid engagement.

How will pricing work?

Sessions, setup projects, and continuing support will be quoted to match your needs. The quote will explain what’s included, any preparation or follow-up, and separate software costs. Additional work requires agreement first.

Who owns the tools and accounts?

You keep control of your accounts and subscriptions. We agree which information and permissions are needed, and you receive instructions for using what we set up.

What happens as AI changes?

Continuing support can include check-ins, new training, troubleshooting, and workflow updates. We agree the time and access included; larger changes are scoped separately.
